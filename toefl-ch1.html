正确答案 A — 词汇题靠语境，不靠背释义。后一句给出了具体内容：缩短渔期没有减少捕获量，反而制造了更危险的抢捕。这是「与意图相反的结果」。B、C、D 在上下文里都没有支撑。

  1. A. most vessels are unaware of the depletion

  3. C. environmental costs are impossible to measure
  4. D. governments refuse to enforce existing rules

3. What can be inferred about quota systems from paragraphs 3 and 4?

  1. A. They have eliminated overfishing in most regions.
  2. B. They changed fishermen's incentives but do not address ecological pressures.
  3. C. They are less effective than closed seasons.
  4. D. They were introduced before minimum net-mesh rules.

4. Which sentence best expresses the essential information in: Whether a stock can maintain itself under such conditions depends less on fishing pressure alone than on its capacity for adaptation.

  1. A. Fishing pressure no longer affects fish populations.
  2. B. A stock's survival now depends more on its ability to adapt than on fishing pressure by itself.

  4. D. Stocks that cannot adapt should not be fished at all.
正确答案 B — 句子简化题要保留原句的比较关系。less A than B = 更取决于 B。A 把 less 读成了 not（绝对化），C 抹掉了比较，D 加入了原句没有的建议。

1. Why does the author say the common description of photosynthesis is misleading?

  1. A. Leaves do not actually store chemical energy.
  2. B. Its energy conversion rate is low; what is impressive is the conditions it operates under.
  3. C. Photosynthesis requires rare metals that are not widely available.
  4. D. Artificial systems have already surpassed it in every respect.
正确答案 B — 第一段的结构是「否定—修正」：先说效率其实很低，再用 What is remarkable is… 指出真正值得称道的是运行条件。B 完整覆盖了这两半。只答前半或只答后半都不够。

2. The phrase prone to in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to

  1. A. protected from
  2. B. likely to suffer from
  3. C. designed to resist
  4. D. unaffected by
正确答案 B — prone to = 易于遭受（负面结果）。句子后半 most prototypes degrade within days 给出了方向：这是坏事。A、C、D 三个干扰项都把方向弄反了——词汇题先判方向，往往就能排掉多数选项。

3. According to paragraph 4, replacing platinum with cobalt and nickel is important mainly because it

  1. A. raises the conversion efficiency of the device
  2. B. makes the technology economically viable
  3. C. eliminates the need for a semiconductor
  4. D. allows the device to work without sunlight
正确答案 B — 原文直接说 Neither change increases raw conversion efficiency, but both improve the economics。A 是被明确否定的内容，属于高危干扰项：它用了原文出现过的词，但站在了反面。

4. What does the author present as the strongest case for artificial photosynthesis?

  2. B. It produces a fuel that can be stored.

正确答案 B — 末句的 not X, but Y 结构直接给出答案：优势不在于胜过太阳能板，而在于产出可储存的燃料。A 恰好被 sceptics 那句否定。
